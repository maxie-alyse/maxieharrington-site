Case Study — Operations · 2024–

Building Story.

A film studio doesn't scale on taste alone. This is the part of the work that doesn't end up on screen — growing Story Co. from a founder and a handful of collaborators into a fifteen-person studio with a sister company.

01 — 2024

A founder and a handful of us.

I joined when Story was mostly conviction. The first job was making the studio real underneath the work — contracts, finance, hiring, production infrastructure — so that every new film started from solid ground instead of from scratch.

02 — Premieres

Proof, in theater seats.

We premiere films the way they deserve. Four premieres, every one sold out — including 960 people at the Palace of Fine Arts for Planet. Venue, ticketing, run-of-show, merch, crowd: produced end to end, in house.

03 — The machine

Systems that let creatives create.

Hiring, procurement, immigration, client delivery — built into processes that run without being chased. The point of operations at a studio is simple: the creative work should never wait on paperwork.

04 — 2026

Fifteen people. A sister company. Sharper every film.

Growth that compounds instead of bloats: The Greatest Lie was made for a quarter of the time and budget of Planet — deliberately. That's what an operational foundation buys a studio: speed, without losing the standard.

Operations is a creative discipline.
